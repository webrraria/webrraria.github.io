News

v0.17.4: Feels Like Home

November 13, 2026

Make a place to come back to. Craft a Work Bench, then add a Furnace, Iron Anvil, Wooden Table, and Wooden Chair. Right-click a placed station to open its crafting recipes. You can also craft from your inventory while standing nearby. Smelt ore into bars, forge an Iron Axe, and save materials on bricks, platforms, glass, and torches.

Supply caches now hold more supplies and different finds. Deeper caches can include tools and better healing items. Already saved chest contents stay as they are. Trees now meet the grass, and the Brick recipe has the right icon.

Accounts are optional. Choose a username and password, keep your recovery code somewhere safe, and mix character parts with the arrow controls. Pixel editing and PNG upload are under Advanced. Signed-in friends can keep a separate character in each host world. The host must save and keep that world. This is not cloud backup.

You can also grab and drag the sun on the title screen. It will not change the time in your world.

Back up your worlds before updating. Both multiplayer players need v0.17.4. Accounts need the updated room service and gateway; you can still play without signing in.

v0.17.3 - Mods, Building, and Graphics

November 2, 2026

Settings now has Graphics Quality: Lowest, Low, Medium, Good, and Awesome. Lower settings reduce world resolution and particles; the interface stays sharp. Smooth lighting and biome background blending stay on. Caves also reuse terrain chunks and lighting buffers.

Build with Stone Slabs, Sandstone Bricks, Snow Bricks, Marble Blocks, and Granite Blocks. Press B for line or hollow-box building in single-player and G to pick a matching block from your inventory. Inventory search and Deposit matching help with storage.

A mod is one ZIP containing its content, textures, and JavaScript together. Import it from Mods. Scripts run by default in single-player; the “Allow its scripts?” confirmation is optional. The confirmation setting and each mod’s Allow / Block choice are remembered after reload. Only import code you trust.

Keep each mod ZIP with your world backups. Multiplayer players need matching enabled packs and scripts blocked. Existing worlds and the room service still work; no backend update is needed.

v0.16.1 - Chests and smoother play

October 25, 2026

Right-click a chest or supply cache to open its 40 storage slots. Click a chest stack to take it; Shift-click an inventory stack to store it. Empty regular chests before mining them. Supply caches can still be mined, but only their remaining items will drop.

Chest sprites now use the right shape, with an open lid while you use them. Contents are saved with the world and shared in multiplayer. Both players need v0.16.1.

News now goes from newest to oldest, without article shortcut buttons. The minimap terrain, tile frames, and unchanged lighting are cached instead of rebuilt repeatedly. The menu no longer generates an unused world, and its background draws at 30 frames per second. Gameplay timing and the existing graphics settings are unchanged.

v0.16 - Below the Surface

October 25, 2026

There’s more to find underground, and more to make when you get home.

  • Amber ore veins and abandoned camps with supply caches. Mine a cache for amber, torches, gel, and a healing potion.
  • Craft an Amber Broadsword, Amber Pickaxe, Amber Lamp, or Amber Tonic.
  • Build with Ice Bricks, Stone Bricks, and Timber Blocks.
  • Watch for faster-hopping sand slimes in the desert and tougher cave zombies underground. These enemies appear in Single Player.
  • Search crafting recipes by item or material name. Four new achievements bring the total to 41.

New ore deposits and camps need a new world. Your old worlds are not regenerated; sand slimes and cave zombies can still provide amber in them. Both multiplayer players need v0.16 for the new blocks and items. Room codes and Manual Invite / Answer still work; the room service does not need another deployment.

About the beta

October 18, 2026

This is a beta, expect to have bugs and stuff!

Webrraria is a small browser game inspired by Terraria. Things may break, and some features are missing. Invite-only multiplayer is in beta: one host and one guest can explore, mine, build, and craft together. Multiplayer has chat and a host-controlled friendly fire option. Enemies are not enabled in multiplayer yet.

Getting started

Choose Single Player → New World, give your world a name, then create it. Move with WASD or the arrow keys and jump with Space. Left-click to dig or attack, right-click to build, and press I or Tab to open your inventory. Esc opens the menu. Phones and tablets are not supported at this moment.

Making a resource pack

  1. Make a folder for your edited PNG files. Use your own work or files you have permission to use.
  2. Keep the original image dimensions and sprite-sheet layout, including the spacing between frames. Edit the pictures without moving the frames around.
  3. Keep the filenames the game recognizes: Tiles_0.png for dirt, Tiles_1.png for stone, and Tiles_2.png for grass. You can start with just one file.
  4. Open Workshop from the menu. Click the file box and select your files together, or drag them into it. Use Mapping Guide for more filenames.

Select the files themselves, not a ZIP or folder. PNGs work directly; XNB files need converting first. Packs change how things look, not the game’s code.

Keeping your worlds

The game saves while you play unless you turn autosave off in Settings. You can also use Save or Save & Exit. In Single Player, use Export to keep a .webrraria backup, and Import World to load it later. Export before closing an embedded preview, since it may only keep saves for that session. Terraria .wld files aren’t supported.

Exploration update

New worlds now include jungle and mushroom biomes. Look for new slimes above ground and cave bats below. Collect mushrooms and fiber, craft better tools, and press H to use a healing potion. Existing worlds keep their terrain. Settings now include zoom, difficulty, autosave, day length, and visual options.

Room codes

Six-character room codes are available again through the new service address. Host Room gives you a code; your friend enters it and clicks Join Room. Manual Invite and Manual Answer are still available. Read “How to join your friend” below for both methods.

Chat and friendly fire

In multiplayer, press Enter or T to chat. Enter sends your message, and Esc closes the input. Friendly Fire starts off. The host can turn it on from the in-game button or Multiplayer menu. Sword attacks can then hurt the other player. Defeated players respawn with their items. Phones and tablets are not supported at this moment.

Not an official Terraria game

We’re not affiliated with Re-Logic or the original Terraria team. Webrraria is an unofficial fan project, not an official port or a replacement for Terraria. Terraria belongs to Re-Logic.

How to join your friend

October 18, 2026

One player hosts the world, and one friend joins. You can use a six-character room code or exchange a Manual Invite and Manual Answer.

Quick connection: room code

Host: make a world in Single Player first, or choose a saved world in Multiplayer. Enter your name and click Host Room. Wait for the six-character code, click Copy Code, and send it privately to your friend. Keep this tab open.

Friend: Save & Exit your own world first. Open Multiplayer, enter your name, paste the six-character code, and click Join Room. There is no answer to send back. The code expires after ten minutes and admits one guest. If joining fails, close it and use a fresh room code.

The service address is already set in this build. Both players need the updated game. If the room service cannot be reached, use the manual method below.

Manual connection

1. Host: send an invite

Make a world in Single Player first, or use an existing saved world. Open Multiplayer, enter your name, choose the world, and click Create Manual Invite. Wait for the code in the lower box, then click Copy My Code and send the whole code privately to your friend. Keep this tab open.

2. Friend: send an answer back

If you are playing your own world, use Save & Exit first. Open Multiplayer and enter your name. Paste the host’s whole invite into Paste your friend’s invite or answer (the upper box), then click Create Manual Answer. Wait for your answer in the lower box, click Copy My Code, and send that whole answer back to the host. Keep your tab open too.

3. Host: connect the answer

Paste your friend’s answer into the upper box and click Connect Answer (host). The world transfers and you both enter the game automatically. You do not need to deploy a room service or leave PowerShell open for this method.

While playing

The host keeps the world and saves shared changes. Signed-out guests have session-only items. Signed-in guests keep a separate character in each host world, saved by that host. Keep a backup of the host world. Press Enter or T to chat. Friendly Fire starts off; only the host can change it. If someone leaves, create a fresh room code or exchange a fresh manual invite and answer to reconnect.

Use the same current game version, copy each code completely, and keep both game tabs open. Some networks may still need a TURN relay. Phones and tablets cannot play at this moment.

Multiplayer

Host a world and share its six-character code, or enter a friend’s code to join.

Codes expire after 10 minutes and admit one guest.

Host a saved world and share its six-character code. Your friend enters it and joins. No answer needs to be sent back. Save & Exit your own world before joining.
